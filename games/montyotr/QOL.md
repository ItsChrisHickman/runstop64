# Monty on the Run: changes from the original

This is the 1985 Commodore 64 game as it was, a native port from the original's own code and data (checked frame
for frame against it; `re/README.md`). **ORIGINAL or ENHANCED:** one switch, E on the pause screen; ENHANCED is the
default and turns on the rows marked ENHANCED, quality-of-life changes for a modern playthrough. ORIGINAL is the game as
it was. Rows marked Always are in both modes.

| Feature | The original | In this remake | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Title screen** | The attract screen: the big scroller (MONTY, ON, THE RUN !, the credits), the MONTY High Scores box rolling through the table of 50, the freedom kit carousel, Rob Hubbard's tune. | The same screen, ported from the original's code and checked frame for frame against it (all but the kit's part, below); its scroller has one more passage before GOOD LUCK!: the credits it leaves out (Peter Harrap, Mark Rodgers, Rob Hubbard, and this tribute). | Always |
| **Freedom kit selection** | On the title, pick 5 items from a carousel of 21 (left/right turn it, down picks); the wrong ones make the game impossible to finish, with no hint which are right. | Nothing to pick: Monty carries the five that work (jet pack, rope, passport, gas mask, barrel of rum). No carousel and no item number on the purple table: MONTY'S FREEDOM KIT stands still under the table, the five icons (the original's graphics) between its pillars, and the rows above the table (where the original showed MONTY FREEDOM KIT. and the picked items) hold ENTER CONTINUE. | Always |
| **High scores** | After a game, a score good enough goes into the table (CONGRATULATIONS !!, PLEASE ENTER YOUR NAME; RETURN puts it in, DEL rubs out; some names do something, "I WANT TO CHEAT" turns on cheat mode); lost when the C64 is switched off. | No name entry (Chris: after a death it was almost the biggest punishment of all): a game ends on CONTINUE? (below). The table is the original's as it ships, rolling in the title's box as attract mode; the HUD's HI-SCORE is the best score since the page was opened, or the table's top. | Always |
| **Pause screen** (P, Esc, pad Start, the touch PAUSE button) | P paused and muted. | The runstop64 pause screen: a map of the whole world, how you're doing (lives, rooms, items, cakes), the options. | Always |
| **World map** | None. | Every room shown: dark grey until visited, then its area's colour; your room flashes; rooms with everything collected shimmer in the original coin colours. The picked room's name and what's left to collect there ("2 coins and 1 item left"); under the map, the freedom kit and the key items found. | Always |
| **Travel** | None. | From the map, travel to any room you've visited (not the ending): arrows pick, Enter (or fire) goes; on a touch screen tap a room, tap it again to go. Monty arrives where he first came in, with the original teleport effect. From the ending too: on the dock, from the moment the treasure takes him there until he walks aboard the boat, P opens the pause screen and travel takes him back to any room visited, the ending undone (the treasure is still there, so coming back to it plays the ending again; once he's aboard the finale plays on uninterrupted, `test/ending.ts`). Driving out of the C5 street leaves it and every room before it behind him for good in the original (room $33 comes off the exit grid); travel goes back across it anyway, with the street as the game has it on that side: to a room before it as before the drive, to a room after it as the drive leaves it, and into the street's rooms with the C5 as it came in furthest along the drive, so a crash can be retried from any room reached. The autosave and back to room start keep the C5 and the street too (`test/c5.ts`) | Always |
| **Back to room start** | None. | B on the pause screen: back to how things were when Monty came into this room. | Always |
| **Back to the title** | None (only by losing, or a restart). | T on the pause screen (or a tap on it): the title, with the save kept; ENTER CONTINUE goes back to it, and only a new game started there begins a new one (keeping the save as the previous one). A new game over a save asks first: NEW GAME? (Space or fire again yes, Esc or a tap no, Enter continues); a jump or fire still held as the last life goes brings CONTINUE? up instead of starting one. | Always |
| **Continue** | Lose your last life: game over, back to the start. | The game over plays as it did; then the title asks "CONTINUE?" straight away (Enter yes, Esc no, or a tap; it waits for an answer), the title's tune behind it: Monty is back in the last room he entered, with five lives and the score from nothing. | Always |
| **After the ending** | France: the boat sails, GAME OVER, the title; the next game starts from nothing. | The finale plays as it did (ENHANCED: then the reward below); then CONTINUE?, and the save is kept (nothing found is lost): yes takes Monty back into the treasure's room as he came into it, with everything he had (coins, items, the freedom kit, lives, score, the map's rooms), to collect what's left; the treasure is still there, so the ending can be played again whenever he likes. No (Esc) leaves ENTER CONTINUE on the title. The pause screen shows how far he's got (rooms, items, cakes; each room's coins and items left on the map). `test/ending.ts` | Always |
| **Autosave** | None. | Progress is saved in your browser every time Monty enters a room; the title offers ENTER CONTINUE (in the row the title leaves empty) or Space for a new game. Kept when the game ends in France too. Launched with a save, the game goes straight back into play where it was (the title only without one, or with T on the pause screen). Starting a new game keeps the save from before it as the previous one: the saved games (F1 on the directory, or the gear there) bring it back, or delete a save (they ask first). | Always |
| **Smooth movement** | Locked to a 50 Hz PAL television. | Game logic still runs at the original 50 Hz; sprites glide between frames on 60/120/144 Hz screens. V switches to whole-pixel steps. | Smooth |
| **Sound** | Music *or* sound effects (the C= key on the title). | Same choice, M to switch, played by Rob Hubbard's original driver. | Music |
| **ENHANCED: only Monty is hit in the jetpack rooms** | In "The Ultimate Experience" Monty's jetpack, hanging on the side he faces away from, kills him when it touches an enemy, just as he does. In the second room the up-and-down enemy's path leaves a 16-pixel gap at the corridor's left wall: Monty fits at one column, and only facing right; steer left to the wall and he turns, the jetpack swings into the enemy, and there's "no room". | With ENHANCED on, only Monty himself is hit there (his jetpack touching an enemy doesn't count): hold left against the wall and fly up past it. Everything else in those rooms is the original (paths, speeds, timings; Monty's own touch still kills). The jetpack no longer picks up items either (Monty does). Checked by `test/jetpack.ts`, against the original program too. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: the C5 street (a crash restarts the room; hold fire for a longer hop)** | A crash (or an enemy) anywhere on the C5 street sends Monty back to the start of the section, by the tree in the first room: all three passes again. The C5's hop is fixed: 7 pixels up, 26 frames in the air. The holes come in sets about a hop apart, so the next hop's press has to come the moment the last one lands: on the return (the middle road), room $33's big hole and pair and then room $32's first pair, right at the edge he drives in from, leave 2 frames for each press at full speed. | With ENHANCED on, Monty starts again in the room he crashed in, where he came into it, the C5 stopped there on the road: standing still is safe in every room of every pass, and every room can be driven through from the stop (`test/c5respawn.ts`). A life still goes. And fire held past the top of a hop holds it there, up to 14 frames (40 in the air), coming down when fire is let go; a tap is the original's hop. On the return at full speed the windows for each press go from 2-10 frames to 8-24 (at least double), and none is smaller anywhere; the holes, enemies, speeds and the hop's height are the original's. Checked by `test/c5.ts` in every C5 room and `test/c5hop.ts` (every hole's window at each speed, ORIGINAL as measured) | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: ride the cloud** | The rising cloud is made of characters that jump 8 pixels at a time, so Monty drops through it unless you keep hopping. | ENHANCED: Monty stands on the cloud and rides it to the top. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: runstop64's rooms** | Right of the start room there's nothing: Monty can't walk on. | With ENHANCED on, walking right from the start goes on into ten new rooms, built from the game's own parts in its own formats (`src/data/runstop64-rooms.ts`, `re/README.md`): the dev room (RUNSTOP in the game's tiles, A RUNSTOP64 TRIBUTE, a waving hand, coins), a rope down to a hub, three warp gates (the game's teleporters, signed: The Ultimate Experience with the jetpack on, the C5 street by the tree, Pie Are Square; each lands as walking in there does) and a playground loop (a pool with a leaping fish, two Mole Crushers, a vault). At most two coins a room (50 points each) and pieces of cake in five of them (the original's cupcake, 200 points); every coin and cake can be had without touching an enemy. On the map in blue (RUNSTOP64 in the legend), not counted in the rooms; travel goes to them. A save made in them, continued in ORIGINAL, starts him in the start room. In ORIGINAL there are none (the original's data is untouched). Checked by `test/runstop64-rooms.ts` | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: no death loops** | A death starts Monty again where he came into the room: if that's in a fall into something deadly, or on an enemy's path, every life goes the same way. | With ENHANCED on, a death before he's had two seconds on his feet in the room starts him instead where he last came into a room and had them (the room before, usually): a life lost once, not every one. Checked by `test/warps.ts` (every warp, and travel there: idle, and right or left held a while: at most one life lost, none after). | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: the reward for winning** | The finale plays (Monty sails for France, GAME OVER) and the game is over; nothing more to see. | After the game's own ending, which plays as it did, the show: a montage of rooms from the journey (the house, the escape tunnel, the sewerage works, the jetpack, the tree stump, the C5 street, the boat, the dock), each one the game's own engine running with its creatures and crushers moving and Monty driven by input like a player's, doing something there (taking an item, collecting coins, driving the C5, taking the treasure and sailing); credits typing on a line at a time in the empty row under the playfield (the makers, true facts, then the port), never on a box; then the parade of the game's creatures, their own sprites and frames, a plain name under each, Monty last; then THE END with the pause screen's numbers (score, lives, rooms, items, cakes), Rob Hubbard's main tune all through, and back to the title (CONTINUE? as after any ending). A key or a tap skips it after two seconds. That the game has been won is kept apart from the saves (a new game never wipes it); once won, W on the pause screen shows it again, and `?ending` on the page shows it at once. Sources of every credit line: `re/credits.md`. Checked by `test/reward.ts` | ENHANCED |
| **Controls** | Joystick or keyboard (and R on the title to choose the keys). | Arrows/WASD, Space (or Z, X) to jump and to start, Enter to confirm, gamepads. The keys are fixed. | Always |
| **Touch controls** (phones, tablets) | None. | 4-way stick, JUMP; PAUSE opens the map (tap a room to pick it, tap it again to travel; tap an option to change it); RETURN key, HOME. Landscape with the controls beside the picture (even with rotation locked), or upright. See docs/mobile.md. | On a touch screen |
| **A moment's grace** | None. | For 3 seconds (150 frames) after the game puts you back into play, nothing kills Monty (an enemy, the piledriver, the lift, the water, the crusher, the C5's crash; the treasure's ending still plays): continuing a save (ENTER CONTINUE, a launch going straight back into play, CONTINUE? after losing), back to room start, arriving from the map. Quality of life for a modern playthrough, so a resume never starts in the middle of a fight; no sign on screen. Both modes. | Always |

## Not changed

Everything else is the original: all 52 of its rooms and their decorations, every
enemy's path and speed, Monty's jump arc, the Mole Crushers, lifts,
teleporters, the jetpack (but for ENHANCED's hit above) and C5 sections (but for ENHANCED's restart and held hop above), the item puzzles, the six death
animations, the game over, the ending, and the title screen's look, scroller,
table and music.
