# Wanted: Monty Mole Remix: changes from the originals

This is **Wanted: Monty Mole** (Gremlin Graphics, ZX Spectrum, 1984: Peter Harrap's) played as **Monty on the Run** (Gremlin Graphics, Commodore 64, 1985) plays: Wanted's 21 rooms, coal, tools, creatures' paths, gates, crushers and
retractable floors, with Monty on the Run's Monty, creatures, look and music. A native rebuild like the others (`docs/montymole-remix-plan.md`): Wanted's own data and rules from `games/montymole`, Monty and the picture from
`games/montyotr`, put together in a folder of their own. "The original" below is Wanted for the world and Monty on the Run for Monty; the faithful Spectrum game is still at `#montymole`. Anything that changes how the game *plays* or
looks (or helps) beyond that is in ENHANCED, one switch (ENHANCED by default; ORIGINAL is the remix as designed, without the helps); the rest is in both.

| Feature | The original | In this port | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The rooms** | Wanted's 21 rooms are Spectrum pictures; what is solid is a colour. | The same 32 x 20 cells, each drawn with Monty on the Run's tiles by what it is to Monty (brick and hatched rock for what is solid, thin ledges for what holds him from above, ropes, water) in the colour the Spectrum tile had; every exit is where it was. Checked by `test/retile.ts`. | Always |
| **Water and fire** | Anything of Monty's that touches a deadly colour kills him: he is never in water, whatever is under it. | Monty on the Run's hazard is the cell under his feet, so a lake one cell deep over a floor (the bottoms of the pipe and castle caverns, the shallows of rooms 8 to 11) could be waded along inside; every solid cell under water, down the column, is water too (`src/fixes.ts`), which puts the deadly cell where the original's contact does. | Always |
| **The ferries** | Anything drawn under his feet holds him, so a creature with a flat top is a place to stand: the slabs that fly along the pipe and castle caverns' lakes (rooms 12 to 16) are how a lake is crossed. | The same creatures, drawn as Wanted draws them (not as a Monty on the Run creature: the picture is what holds him), hold him where their top row has a pixel (`isFerry`, `holdFromCreature`); he rides one by walking with it. Checked by the bot (`test/passable.ts`: room 12 to 13 across the lake). | Always |
| **Where he comes in at a side** | A way in at the left puts him at x 2, at the right x $EE. | The same (Monty on the Run puts him a gutter's width further out, 14 pixels off the room, which would have put him past the first ferry). | Always |
| **Monty** | Wanted: a vertical jump or a 32-pixel jump over 1.28 s, steered by nothing; four rows of fall (32 pixels) kill. Monty on the Run: a somersault jump 38 pixels across, 20 up, 0.76 s; no fall death. | Monty on the Run's Monty, from the code both games share (`games/shared/monty`): his walk (50 pixels a second, as Wanted's), the somersault jump, ropes, his frames and his death (a dissolve, a sink, a crusher's). No death from a fall. Checked by `test/game.ts`. | Always |
| **The coal** | Wanted's piece in the room's picture (a lump in the mine, other things in the castle), flickering through the colours; 64 pieces, worth what the programmer's source left (3392 in all); the score is the coal. | The same picture, pixel for pixel, as a C64 character flickering through the C64's colours, counted and worth the same. Not Monty on the Run's coins. | Always |
| **The tools** | One a room at most (a pick, a club, scissors, a candle-whip...), carried until the room is left or he dies; one touch of a creature then kills the creature, not Monty. | Each room's own object, Wanted's picture of it drawn as a C64 sprite, taken by touching it; the same rule. | Always |
| **The creatures** | Wanted's 16 x 16 pictures walking its records' paths (legs, turns, relays that time the next). | The paths and timing are Wanted's own (the faithful game's rules, run on Monty on the Run's frames: 25 passes a second inside the 50 Hz frame). Each kind is drawn as the Monty on the Run creature that fits its room (`src/kinds.ts`); four animals nothing there is (the duck, the dog, the squirrel, the castle's chess horse) keep Wanted's own frames in C64 colours. | Always |
| **The crushers** | A picture drawn over the room: its shaft from just under the ceiling to the head, going down, the head and the rows that wipe the shaft going up; it waits at the top a random while; Monty stands on it. | The same picture, line for line (`src/crusher-picture.ts`), as characters in the room's own cell grid; where it has a pixel on a cell's top row it holds him from above. The crusher's record, its random waits and every line drawn equal the faithful game's every frame in all nine rooms (`test/crusher.ts`). Its death is Monty on the Run's. | Always |
| **The gates** | Rooms 6, 11, 15 and 20 open at exactly 16, 32, 48 and 64 pieces taken, but taking the piece that makes the count outside the gate's own room shuts it for good (so a game can be played so a gate never opens). | A gate opens when its count is reached, wherever he is, and is open when he comes to it. The one rule of Wanted's changed: a mend, in both modes. | Always |
| **Lives** | Wanted: three, none gained. Monty on the Run: five. | Five. | Always |
| **The title** | Wanted: a loading picture and Colonel Bogey. Monty on the Run: the big scroller, the rolling box of high scores, the purple table. | Monty on the Run's title (one code for both: `games/montyotr/src/title.ts`) with the logo WANTED: MONTY MOLE REMIX in its lettering and both games' credits in its scroller; its tune. No freedom kit under the table (there is none), so ENTER CONTINUE has the rows over it. | Always |
| **Music** | Wanted: beeper tunes. Monty on the Run: Rob Hubbard's SID music and effects. | Monty on the Run's, on the title and in the game, from the same SID file and player code; M switches between the music and the sound effects. | Always |
| **Pause screen** (P, Esc, pad Start, the touch PAUSE button) | None in either. | The runstop64 pause screen in C64 colours: the map of the 21 rooms, how you're doing (lives, rooms come to, coal taken, score), the ORIGINAL / ENHANCED switch. | Always |
| **Map** | Wanted: none. | The 21 rooms laid out as Wanted's world is (the shaft down the left, the tunnel along the bottom, the caverns, the castle), each a small picture of itself in the remix's colours, dark until you have come to it; where you are flashes; a room whose coal is all taken has a green frame; under the grid the picked room and how many of its pieces of coal are taken. | Always |
| **Travel** | None. | From the map, go to any room you have come to: arrows pick, Enter (or jump) goes; on a touch screen tap a room, tap it again to go. You arrive at the place you first came into it. | Always |
| **Back to room start** | None. | B on the pause screen: back to how things were when Monty came into this room (lives, coal and score as then). | Always |
| **Back to the title** | None (only by losing). | T on the pause screen (or a tap on it): the title, with the save kept; ENTER CONTINUE goes back to it, and only a new game started there begins a new one (keeping the save as the previous one). A new game over a save asks first: NEW GAME? | Always |
| **Continue** | Lose the last life: GAME OVER and a new game from nothing. | After the last life, the title asks "CONTINUE?" straight away (Enter yes, Esc no, or a tap): Monty is back at the start of the room he was in, with five lives and the coal and score he had. | Always |
| **Autosave** | None. | Progress is saved in your browser each time Monty enters a room (after a death too: the lives lost stay lost). Launched with a save, the game opens on its title with ENTER CONTINUE. Starting a new game keeps the save from before it as the previous one: the saved games (F1 on the directory, or the gear there) bring it back. | Always |
| **Touch controls** (phones, tablets) | None. | 4-way stick and JUMP; PAUSE opens the map (tap a room to pick it, tap it again to travel). Landscape with the controls beside the picture (even with rotation locked), or upright. See docs/mobile.md. | On a touch screen |
| **A moment's grace** | None. | For 3 seconds (150 frames) after the game puts you back into play (continuing a save, back to room start, arriving from the map), a death does not happen, so a resume never starts in the middle of a mishap. No sign on screen. Both modes. | Always |
| **ENHANCED: unlimited lives and continues** | Five lives; the last one lost is GAME OVER. | A death takes no life: the lives stay as they are, so the game never ends and never asks CONTINUE?. He is still put back as the game puts him, and nothing makes him invincible. For a modern playthrough of a classic; the same in every game. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: no death loops** | A death starts Monty again where he came into the room: if that is a fall onto something fatal, or on a creature's path, every life goes the same way. | A death before he has had a moment on his feet (100 frames) in the room starts him instead where he last came into a room and had that moment (the room before, usually): a life lost once, not every one. A death after the moment is as designed. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: coal to the next gate** | Four gates open at 16, 32, 48 and 64 pieces taken, and nothing says how many that is; the score is the coal's worth, not its count. | The row under the room, which is empty, says `COAL TO GATE: 7` in the score row's letters: the pieces left before the next gate's count; not shown once all four have counted. Drawn into the game's own screen memory; changes nothing in the game. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: sparkles on the tool** | Each room's object lies unmarked. | The game's state puts stardust (c64kit's sparkles, as in Moley Christmas and Wanted, over the picture, no text or boxes) on the room's tool while it lies there and he carries none. Changes nothing in the game. | ENHANCED |

## Not changed

Everything else is Wanted's: the 21 rooms and their coal (64 pieces, 3392 points), the tools and what they do, every creature's path and the relays that time them, the crushers with their random waits, the retractable
floors, room 16's pit that lifts Monty into the castle, the gates at 16, 32, 48 and 64. Everything of Monty's is Monty on the Run's. **Still to come** (`docs/montymole-remix-plan.md`, stage 6): the opening scene (a new game starts in room 0 as the
scene leaves him, with the coal already in the rooms), room 16's lift as a scene, and the ending and its reward.
