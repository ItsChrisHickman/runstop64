# Do Svidaniya Monty: changes from Monty on the Run

Do Svidaniya Monty is a new game (2026, `docs/dosvidaniya-plan.md`) on the engine, tiles, sprites and music of **Monty on the Run** (Gremlin Graphics, Commodore 64, 1985): the engine in `games/montyotr/src` takes its game data as a value, and this game is a second data set (`games/montyworld/src/data.ts`), not a copy. "The original" below is Monty on the Run. It is in the directory (Chris played Moscow and liked it; `#montyworld`), and only its first region, Moscow, is built so far: nine rooms from the station where Monty arrives to the roof of the Trans-Siberian's carriage, where the way east is shut with a sign until Siberia is built. Anything that changes how the game *plays* or looks (or helps) beyond Monty on the Run's own is in ENHANCED, one switch (ENHANCED by default; ORIGINAL is the game as designed, without the helps); the rest is in both.

| Feature | The original | In this port | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The world** | 52 rooms on a grid of 23 by 6 cells. | Rooms of our own on a grid of 50 by 3 cells (seven regions, west to east: for now Moscow's nine rooms, a block four wide and three high, each its own shape and way through: two lanes crossed at different heights, ropes through floors and ceilings, a dead end with an item), made of Monty on the Run's rules, music and what of its tiles fit, and new tiles, decorations and creatures in its style. The pause screen's map shows the region Monty is in, its cells large. | Always |
| **Monty and the rules** | Walk, somersault jump, ropes, coins (50), items (200), Mole Crushers, lifts, teleporters, five lives, the deaths. | The same code (`games/shared/monty`, `games/montyotr/src`): Moscow has two Mole Crushers (the engine's own). | Always |
| **Escalators** | None. | Drawn as escalators (`art/escalator.ts`): a band at 45 degrees with a handrail whose dashes and steps (a sawtooth along the band) run along it in the way it goes, as fast as Monty is carried. A ride you cannot leave, as the lift and the crusher's head are: step on the foot and the treads carry Monty along the slope (a pixel in 1.5 frames; the hall's goes down 5 cells to a terrace over the lower floor, the platform's up 8, 2 seconds) and set him down on the landing, whatever he holds. From the other end it is never ignored either: walking onto the landing's far side against the way it goes, the treads carry him back to the landing (he walks in place while they push him, over and over while he holds the stick); to leave the head of the platform's escalator towards the gallery he jumps. Data: `escalators` (with `treads`, the pictures the band's characters take in turn) in the game's value. | Always |
| **Icicles** | None. | Seen all the time, hanging from an eave. When Monty's width is over one's column it shivers for 24 frames (it flickers and shakes: the warning), falls, breaks and grows back (60 frames, not armed while it does). Running straight under one is safe (he is out of its column before it falls); stopping or turning back under one is not: it kills as a crusher's head does. Data: `icicles`. | Always |
| **Key items open the way** | The special items change the rooms (a wall goes, a creature is seen off). | The metro token (the gum arcade's dead end) takes the metro hall's turnstile bars away; the ushanka (the platform's lane, behind a crusher and the turnstile) sends the Kremlin guard off the wall (`roomEffects`, as the original's). | Always |
| **Costumes** | Monty always looks the same. | A costume is a key item whose pickup puts it on him, kept in the save. Moscow's is the ushanka, a fur hat in fur brown over his one-colour figure: a sprite of its own (sprite 7, a room's fourth creature's: Moscow has none) that follows each of his 36 pictures (walk, rope, somersault, both ways: the hat turned with each somersault picture), with his own picture cleared where it sits and a black line round it (`costumes` with `over`, `art/costume.ts`, `art/hat.ts`). The crown stands above his head, the fur brim comes down on his forehead, the ear flaps hang at the sides of his face. It is no part of his collision box. Each region has a costume, in place of the last. | Always |
| **Pirozhki** | The cupcakes (200 points each). | Moscow's two cakes are pirozhki, small baked buns drawn as the cupcake is (the same sprite format and colour, the same 200 points); the pause screen counts SNACKS, the word for every region's food (`progress.foodName`; Monty on the Run's says CAKES). The panel's lines are one width, the numbers right-aligned in a column (LIVES, ROOMS, ITEMS and the food), in Monty on the Run's pause screen as well. | Always |
| **Pause screen, map, travel, back to room start, autosave, continue, grace** | The pause screen and map are ours in Monty on the Run. | The same, for this game's rooms. | Always |
| **Saves** | None (ours). | Its own codec (`CODEC` in `src/main.ts`; the save is in the envelope `{ v: 3, game: "montyworld", format: 1 }`): Monty on the Run's shape with what he wears. | Always |
| **ENHANCED: unlimited lives and continues** | Five lives, then game over. | A death takes no life (`games/shared/lives.ts`). | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: safe respawn** | A death starts Monty again where he came into the room. | A death before he has had a moment on his feet there starts him again where he last came into a room and had that moment. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: item hints** | The items' uses are for the player to find. | A key item picked up says what it is for in the name row (TOKEN: FOR THE METRO TURNSTILE, FUR HAT: PAST THE KREMLIN GUARD) for four seconds, and coming into a room that wants one he has not got, once the room's name has had its two seconds, says so (THE TURNSTILE WANTS A TOKEN, THE GUARD WANTS A FUR HAT). Auf Wiedersehen Monty's ITEM HINTS say the same on its panel (`games/shared/monty/hints.ts`). | ENHANCED |

## Not changed

Monty's walk and jump, the coins and items and what they score, the enemies' paths and speeds as the rooms give them, Rob Hubbard's tune and sound effects (Moscow's own version of the tune is made apart), the title screen's frame and lettering (the scroller says MONTY MOLE: WORLD TOUR with its credits).
