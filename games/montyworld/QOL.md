# Do Svidaniya Monty: changes from Monty on the Run

Do Svidaniya Monty is a new game (2026, `docs/dosvidaniya-plan.md`) on the engine, tiles, sprites and music of **Monty on the Run** (Gremlin Graphics, Commodore 64, 1985): the engine in `games/montyotr/src` takes its game data as a value, and this game is a second data set (`games/montyworld/src/data.ts`), not a copy. "The original" below is Monty on the Run. It is in the directory (Chris played Moscow and liked it; `#montyworld`), and six regions are built so far: Moscow (nine rooms, from the station where Monty arrives to the roof of the Trans-Siberian's carriage), Siberia (nine rooms, from that roof to the border village), China (nine rooms, from the Great Wall's foot to the junk on the river) Japan (eleven rooms, from the fishing harbour where the junk lands to the cargo ship's gangway) and the Pacific (seventeen rooms, from the cargo ship's stern through Oahu, Molokai, Maui and the Big Island to the cargo plane's hold) and the Andes and the Amazon (twelve rooms, from the plane's tail ramp by parachute down through the mountains to Machu Picchu's terraces, the village, the cloud forest and the riverboat's river), where the way east is shut with a sign until Rio is built. Anything that changes how the game *plays* or looks (or helps) beyond Monty on the Run's own is in ENHANCED, one switch (ENHANCED by default; ORIGINAL is the game as designed, without the helps); the rest is in both.

## Not changed

Monty's walk and jump, the coins and items and what they score, the enemies' paths and speeds as the rooms give them, Rob Hubbard's tune and sound effects (each region's own version of the tune is made apart: Moscow's minor with a balalaika, Siberia's Dorian with a pipe, China's pentatonic with a bowed fiddle, Japan's Hirajoshi scale with a shakuhachi and a koto, the Pacific's major scale with a ukulele, the Andes' minor pentatonic with panpipes and a charango, Rio's major with a cavaquinho and an agogo; the tempo is Hubbard's), the title screen's lettering and big scroller (the title is the game's own: the logo over the map from Montos to Rio with Monty walking the route in his ushanka, the story scrolling under it: Intermole finds him on his island of Montos, he runs east out of Europe's reach, Moscow to Rio; the credits at the foot).
