# Do Svidaniya Monty: changes from Monty on the Run

Do Svidaniya Monty is a new game (2026, `docs/dosvidaniya-plan.md`) on the engine, tiles, sprites and music of **Monty on the Run** (Gremlin Graphics, Commodore 64, 1985): the engine in `games/montyotr/src` takes its game data as a value, and this game is a second data set (`games/montyworld/src/data.ts`), not a copy. "The original" below is Monty on the Run. It is in the directory (Chris played Moscow and liked it; `#montyworld`), and three regions are built so far: Moscow (nine rooms, from the station where Monty arrives to the roof of the Trans-Siberian's carriage), Siberia (nine rooms, from that roof to the border village) and China (nine rooms, from the Great Wall's foot to the junk on the river), where the way east is shut with a sign until Japan is built. Anything that changes how the game *plays* or looks (or helps) beyond Monty on the Run's own is in ENHANCED, one switch (ENHANCED by default; ORIGINAL is the game as designed, without the helps); the rest is in both.

| Feature | The original | In this port | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The world** | 52 rooms on a grid of 23 by 6 cells. | Rooms of our own on a grid of 50 by 3 cells (seven regions, west to east: for now Moscow's, Siberia's and China's nine rooms each, a block four wide and three high, each its own shape and way through: two lanes crossed at different heights, ropes through floors and ceilings, a dead end with an item), made of Monty on the Run's rules, music and what of its tiles fit, and new tiles, decorations and creatures in its style. The pause screen's map shows the region Monty is in, its cells large. | Always |
| **Monty and the rules** | Walk, somersault jump, ropes, coins (50), items (200), Mole Crushers, lifts, teleporters, five lives, the deaths. | The same code (`games/shared/monty`, `games/montyotr/src`): Moscow has two Mole Crushers (the engine's own). | Always |
| **Escalators** | None. | Drawn as escalators (`art/escalator.ts`): a band at 45 degrees with a handrail whose dashes and steps (a sawtooth along the band) run along it in the way it goes, as fast as Monty is carried. A ride you cannot leave, as the lift and the crusher's head are: step on the foot and the treads carry Monty along the slope (a pixel in 1.5 frames; the hall's goes down 5 cells to a terrace over the lower floor, the platform's up 8, 2 seconds) and set him down on the landing, whatever he holds. From the other end it is never ignored either: walking onto the landing's far side against the way it goes, the treads carry him back to the landing (he walks in place while they push him, over and over while he holds the stick); to leave the head of the platform's escalator towards the gallery he jumps. Data: `escalators` (with `treads`, the pictures the band's characters take in turn) in the game's value. | Always |
| **Icicles** | None. | Seen all the time, hanging from an eave. When Monty's width is over one's column it shivers for 24 frames (it flickers and shakes: the warning), falls, breaks and grows back (60 frames, not armed while it does). Running straight under one is safe (he is out of its column before it falls); stopping or turning back under one is not: it kills as a crusher's head does. Data: `icicles`. | Always |
| **Key items open the way** | The special items change the rooms (a wall goes, a creature is seen off). | The metro token (the gum arcade's dead end) takes the metro hall's turnstile bars away; the ushanka (the platform's lane, behind a crusher and the turnstile) sends the Kremlin guard off the wall (`roomEffects`, as the original's). | Always |
| **Costumes** | Monty always looks the same. | A costume is a key item whose pickup puts it on him, kept in the save. Moscow's is the ushanka, a fur hat in fur brown over his one-colour figure: a sprite of its own (sprite 7, a room's fourth creature's: Moscow has none) that follows each of his 36 pictures (walk, rope, somersault, both ways: the hat turned with each somersault picture), with his own picture cleared where it sits and a black line round it (`costumes` with `over`, `art/costume.ts`, `art/hat.ts`). The crown stands above his head, the fur brim comes down on his forehead, the ear flaps hang at the sides of his face. It is no part of his collision box. Each region has a costume, in place of the last: Moscow's ushanka stays through Siberia and China's rice hat takes its place. | Always |
| **Tunnel mouths** | None. | A portal of rock six cells wide (two of rock at each side and two at the top, so it reads as a hill with a tunnel through it) and seven tall sweeps a train roof, from the front of the train in the dining car and from each end in the sleeper (a cell every 10 frames, six seconds across), its edge flashing at the entry for the warning (48 frames) first; what of Monty is in it when it reaches him dies (event 1, as a crusher's head), on the roof or up a rope; under the roof (a hatch's rope, the car) nothing happens. What it covers is put back as it passes. He walks a cell in 8 frames, so he cannot run through it but can outrun it. The dining car has one, the sleeper two, at the same pace 190 frames apart, so they cross each other over the same cells each cycle. Data: `tunnels`. | Always |
| **Cracking ice** | None. | Lake Baikal's ice counts the frames Monty stands on a patch (a jump is not standing): lines after 56, wider ones after 88, gone after 120 (he falls into the lake and dies), back whole 240 frames later; off it the count dies away. A patch is six cells; walking across without stopping his body is over it for about 56 frames (measured: 71 frames over eight cells, the patch just showing lines as he leaves), so crossing is safe by a little and waiting is not. Data: `cracks`. | Always |
| **Mine cart** | Monty on the Run's lift. | A lift with a picture of its own (a coal tub on a hoist chain): the mine mouth's cart hangs off the west end of a high shelf (climbed to by timber ledges, a coin on it), clear of the gantry he walks in on, and carries him down to the gallery beside the way on (`lifts` with `look`). | Always |
| **The lamp** | None. | The miner's lamp (the mine mouth's pocket) puts the dark shaft's ledges in place (`roomEffects` with `place`): without it the shaft is dark (dim rock) and the ledges are only felt, as dots, and are not there. The ticket (the tender) lifts the village's border barrier. | Always |
| **Timed strikes** | None. | Cells that are harmless at rest, flicker for 18 or 20 frames (the warning: a lit fuse, an arm twitching), are deadly while they are live (a bang, an arm reaching down; death as a crusher's head), and then show a scrap or nothing until their period comes round, counting from the room's loading. The hutong's string of nine firecrackers goes off from its left end a cell every 6 frames (faster than he walks, a cell in 8), each a bang of 12 frames, and is hung again after 450 frames (first lit after 170, so he meets it on the ledge): he waits until it is spent and crosses while it is. The willow garden's three crouching figures on the bridge reach down with both arms (a column two cells wide from the bridge to the water) over the river's three gaps, a third of a 150-frame period apart, each for 36 frames after 20 of twitching: he waits on a stone and jumps when the arm is back. Data: `pulses`. | Always |
| **The New Year dragon** | None. | Six sprites (the head, four body pieces, the tail: sprites 0, 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6, so its room has no creature, item, icicle or lift) follow one smooth closed path through the market a pixel a frame, each 18 pixels of path behind the one before; the head faces the way it goes and opens and shuts its mouth. The path goes over the top of the room, down the right side, along the street and up in the middle; whatever of it touches him is death. The street is shut while the dragon is on it: a walk along it from the middle to the way out is untouched from about four starts in ten. Data: `dragons`. | Always |
| **Trapdoor, gate, raft** | The special items change rooms (a wall goes, a creature leaves). | The tower key (the hutong's firecracker ledge) takes the plug from the tower's roof and puts the rope's last cells there (`clear` and `place`); the rice hat (the wall's top) takes the city gate's doors away (`clear`); the bamboo (the willow garden's island) puts a raft across the quay's river (`place`). | Always |
| **Dumplings** | The cupcakes. | China's two cakes are dumplings in their steamer (200 points); the pause screen counts them with the pirozhki and the pelmeni as SNACKS. | Always |
| **Pelmeni** | The cupcakes. | Siberia's two cakes are pelmeni, Siberian dumplings (200 points); the pause screen counts them with Moscow's pirozhki as SNACKS. | Always |
| **Pirozhki** | The cupcakes (200 points each). | Moscow's two cakes are pirozhki, small baked buns drawn as the cupcake is (the same sprite format and colour, the same 200 points); the pause screen counts SNACKS, the word for every region's food (`progress.foodName`; Monty on the Run's says CAKES). The panel's lines are one width, the numbers right-aligned in a column (LIVES, ROOMS, ITEMS and the food), in Monty on the Run's pause screen as well. | Always |
| **Pause screen, map, travel, back to room start, autosave, continue, grace** | The pause screen and map are ours in Monty on the Run. | The same, for this game's rooms. | Always |
| **Saves** | None (ours). | Its own codec (`CODEC` in `src/main.ts`; the save is in the envelope `{ v: 3, game: "montyworld", format: 1 }`): Monty on the Run's shape with what he wears. Room ids and table places never change once a region has shipped, so Moscow-only and Siberia saves carry on in the game with China (their shorter coin and item lists fill the first entries; a second costume's blocks write over the first's hat pictures, so a game carried on in either hat shows that one; a game with no C5 street no longer restores its exit grid from a save, which never changes there). The format stays 1: the bytes' shape is the same. | Always |
| **ENHANCED: unlimited lives and continues** | Five lives, then game over. | A death takes no life (`games/shared/lives.ts`). | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: safe respawn** | A death starts Monty again where he came into the room. | A death before he has had a moment on his feet there starts him again where he last came into a room and had that moment. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: item hints** | The items' uses are for the player to find. | A key item picked up says what it is for in the name row (TOKEN: FOR THE METRO TURNSTILE, FUR HAT: PAST THE KREMLIN GUARD, LAMP: SHOWS THE MINE'S LEDGES, TICKET: FOR THE BORDER BARRIER, KEY: FOR THE TOWER'S TRAPDOOR, RICE HAT: PAST THE CITY GATE, BAMBOO: A RAFT OUT TO THE JUNK) for four seconds, and coming into a room that wants one he has not got, once the room's name has had its two seconds, says so (THE TURNSTILE WANTS A TOKEN, THE GUARD WANTS A FUR HAT, TOO DARK TO SEE: FIND A LAMP, THE BARRIER WANTS A TICKET, THE TRAPDOOR WANTS A KEY, THE CITY GATE WANTS A RICE HAT, OUT TO THE JUNK: FIND BAMBOO). Auf Wiedersehen Monty's ITEM HINTS say the same on its panel (`games/shared/monty/hints.ts`). | ENHANCED |

## Not changed

Monty's walk and jump, the coins and items and what they score, the enemies' paths and speeds as the rooms give them, Rob Hubbard's tune and sound effects (each region's own version of the tune is made apart: Moscow's minor with a balalaika, Siberia's Dorian with a pipe, China's pentatonic with a bowed fiddle; the tempo is Hubbard's), the title screen's frame and lettering (the scroller says MONTY MOLE: WORLD TOUR with its credits).
