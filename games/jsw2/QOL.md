# Jet Set Willy II: changes from the original

This is a translated port of the 1985 Commodore 64 game: every routine of the original translated to TypeScript and checked
against the original running on an emulated C64, cycle for cycle (`re/README.md`). **ORIGINAL or ENHANCED:** one switch, E
on the pause screen; ENHANCED is the default and turns on the rows marked ENHANCED, quality-of-life changes for a modern
playthrough. ORIGINAL is the game as it was. Rows marked Always are in both modes.

| Feature | The original | In this port | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Back to room start** | A trap with no way out means starting again. | B on the pause screen: back to how things were on coming into this room (the latest autosave). | Always |
| **Back to the title** | None (only by losing, or a restart). | T on the pause screen (or a tap on it): the title, with the save kept; ENTER CONTINUE goes back to it, and only a new game started there begins a new one (keeping the save as the previous one). A new game over a save asks first: NEW GAME? (Space or fire again yes, Esc or a tap no, Enter continues); a jump or fire still held as the last life goes brings CONTINUE? up instead of starting one. | Always |
| **A new game only on your say** | After the last life the title starts a game on Return as well as Space; a game that began that way (an Enter hit as the title came up) was a new game in the bathroom, with what you had found gone. | Enter on the title with a save continues it from the first moment the title is up, before ENTER CONTINUE is drawn. And a game that begins any way but NEW GAME? answered yes (or with no save to lose) is not taken for a new game: the save carries on, with what you had found (`games/shared/native/play.ts`, `ContinueFlow.newGameAsked`). Checked by `test/wipe.ts`. | Always |
| **Autosave** | None. | Progress is saved in your browser each time Willy enters a room: once he has found his feet there (as travel's spots, below) and lived a second, so a save is never in mid-air or where he dies at once (falling into Hades, the save stays the room he fell from). The title screen offers ENTER CONTINUE (or Space / fire for a new game). Launched with a save, the game goes straight back into play where it was (the title only without one, or with T on the pause screen). Starting a new game keeps the save from before it as the previous one: the saved games (F1 on the directory, or the gear there) bring it back, or delete a save (they ask first). | Always |
| **Continue** | When the last life goes, back to the title. | "CONTINUE?" (Enter yes, Esc no; it waits for an answer): back to the last room entered, with all eight lives. | Always |
| **Pause screen with a map** | F1 pauses on a blank wait. | P, Esc, pad Start or the gear: a map of the whole mansion (136 rooms, laid out from the game's own room data so that every exit is two rooms side by side the way it leads: down is a room below, left a room to the left; the 12 of its 320 exits that cannot be, the mansion is not a consistent grid, are dotted blue connectors from room to room, dim until both rooms have been in), lit as Willy explores, with the room he's in; rooms found, items and lives. Items (the game's own count and per-room bits, read only): the side panel reads ITEMS n / OF 178 and NEED 160 (the Masters Bedroom ends the game once 160 are taken), then GO TO BED from 160 on, when the Masters Bedroom blinks on the map; a room he has been in that still has items shows a cyan dot (ITEMS in the legend), a room with none left is plain, rooms he has not been in show nothing. F1 is left out. | Always |
| **Running** | One speed. | ENHANCED: hold Shift (touch: RUN, on or off) and Willy runs: his own steps come twice as often (walking; a jump half as fast again), the game's own steps, so a jump is the same jump, a fall the same fall, only quicker. The guardians, ropes, conveyors, clock and music keep their pace. Not on a rope (his climb goes with its swing), and not in the pass or two after coming into a room while the game still holds the last room's collisions. Let go and he walks at the game's own pace again. Pause, the map, travel and autosave work as ever. Shift reaches nothing else (not the C64's keyboard). ORIGINAL: Shift does nothing. | ENHANCED, while held |
| **No death loops** | A death in a fall from another room starts Willy again where the fall began: from the hole in Attack of the Mutant Dustybins' floor he falls into Hades onto the skull, is put back at the hole's edge and dies again at once (two lives for one fall; held left, every life goes that way). | ENHANCED: a life lost before he's lived a second on his feet in a room starts him again from the last autosave (where he had), with the lives he has left: one life for the fall into Hades. Checked by `test/deathloop.ts` (with the original's own two lives, on its 6502 code). ORIGINAL keeps the original's rule (the autosave is safe in both). | ENHANCED |
| **Safe landings** | A fall of 40 pixels or more (five character rows: the fall counter $82D7, two a step, against `cmp #$28` at $5465) kills Willy on landing. | ENHANCED: no fall kills; a long fall ends in an ordinary landing and he walks on (the code's own compare, its operand read from the game's memory and set to $FF: the counter only ever counts in twos, so it never gets there). Quality of life for a modern playthrough. What he lands on is the game's own business as ever: a guardian, a nasty or Hades' skull still hurts, and the fall into Hades still costs its life (the skull). Checked by `test/falls.ts`. ORIGINAL: 40, as it was. | ENHANCED |
| **Smooth movement** | Locked to a 50 Hz PAL television. | The game at its own pace; sprites glide between its frames on 60/120/144 Hz screens, except a step taken with a new image after standing still: the game walks its figures inside their sprites (a new image every four frames, each drawn 2 pixels on, then the sprite 8 pixels on with the first image again), and those steps are drawn where the game puts them (glided, the new image went back, then forward: Macaroni Ted, the spaceman, Willy). | Always |
| **Travel back** | None. | On the pause screen: the arrows (or a tap) pick any room he's been in on his feet, Enter (or a second tap) takes him there: the game's own way into a room, arriving where he came in (where the game itself starts him again after a lost life), or, if he came in in the air (dropping in, jumping in), where he landed. A spot is kept once he has lived a second after it (or left the room alive); rooms he never found his feet in, or only died in, are dark brown, NO TRAVEL. What's been found is kept with each autosave. Travel in the middle of a fall (from Hades, say) arrives with the fall forgotten: he drops in above the spot, and a death later starts him there, not in the room the fall began in. | Always |
| **Title screen text** | The scroller credits the original team only in part, and lists the C64's keys (Q/O, W/P, F1). | Credits to the whole team, and this keyboard's keys (Shift to run, and Space again in the air, in ENHANCED); "PRESS SPACE TO START" (Enter continues). | Always |
| **Controls** | Keyboard (Q/O left, W/P right, Space jump) or a joystick in port 2. | Arrows/WASD, Space (or Z, X, pad A) to jump, and again in the air (ENHANCED); gamepads; Shift runs (ENHANCED). F3 music on or off, M mutes. | Always |
| **Touch controls** (phones, tablets) | None. | Left/right stick, JUMP (tap it again in the air: the second jump, in ENHANCED), RUN (on/off: running, in ENHANCED); PAUSE opens the map (tap a room, tap again to go; tap an option to change it); RETURN key, HOME. Landscape with the controls beside the picture (even with rotation locked), or upright. See docs/mobile.md. | On a touch screen |
| **A moment's grace** | None. | For 3 seconds (150 frames) after the game puts you back into play, Willy can't lose a life (the death routine does nothing; nothing else is taken from him): continuing a save (ENTER CONTINUE, a launch going straight back into play, CONTINUE? after losing), back to room start, arriving from the map, and ENHANCED's no-death-loops restart. Quality of life for a modern playthrough, so a resume never starts in the middle of a fight; no sign on screen. Both modes. | Always |
| **A second jump in the air** | None: a jump cannot be changed once begun. | ENHANCED, for a game that is a lot easier: press Space (or Z, X, pad A, JUMP) again while he is in the air, rising or falling, and the jump begins again from the height he is at: the game's own jump (its rise flag and table step put back, no fall), so walls, ceilings and rooms behave as ever. Once per airtime (it comes back on landing, or off a rope). With left or right held he turns that way, as the game does at the start of a jump; with neither he goes on as he was. Checked by `test/easy.ts`. | ENHANCED |
| **Ropes hold** | A rope is taken hold of only where Willy's sprite touches its one dotted pixel, he falls off if that touch is missing for three steps, and the jump off works only on its lower part. | ENHANCED: he takes hold of a rope as soon as he is in its cells, never falls off it, and the jump off works from anywhere on the rope, the way held (left, right or straight up): the game's own rope code, its contact report ($8305, bit 0) set and the jump-off test's operand ($54E2) read from memory. For 0.9 seconds after he lets go he is left to the VIC's own touch, so a jump off takes him away. The beach (room 28) is crossed with the rope and a second jump (`test/easy.ts`). | ENHANCED |
| **Lives that never run out** | Eight lives; the last one lost ends the game. | ENHANCED: the eight Willies are always there (the lives byte filled again at the main loop's wait, after the no-death-loops arrival has seen a life go). | ENHANCED |
| **A grace on coming into a room** | Some arrivals (dropping in beside a guardian, walking in at a nasty) cost a life before any input. | ENHANCED: 2 seconds (100 frames) in which no life can be lost on coming into each room, and again after a life is lost (the room's start); the death routine does nothing, as in the 3-second grace after a resume (above). A hit still there when it ends kills. | ENHANCED |
| **A gentle touch** | The VIC reports a collision for any one pixel two sprites share, so a graze kills. | ENHANCED: a guardian or nasty must share 14 opaque pixels with Willy's sprite (about a quarter of him) to kill: the model's `spriteOverlap`, set from the front end. Ropes and what he stands on are unchanged. In the bathroom a guardian that kills at a gap of 14 pixels in ORIGINAL must be 11 pixels into him in ENHANCED (`test/easy.ts`). | ENHANCED |
| **Sparkles on the items left** | Items must be found by looking. | ENHANCED: a sparkle on each item still in the room he is in (the cell of each, from the room's item table and the game's own bits). The pause map's ITEMS n / OF 178 count and the blinking Masters Bedroom (from 160 on, visited or not) are in both modes. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: the ending** | Taking 160 items to the Masters Bedroom is the game's own end: its code (the room's handler at $7315) jumps through the vector at $01E0, which in the copy this port is made from comes back to the bedroom's own routine, so Willy drops in again and again (there is no end screen to show). | A reward for finishing: when he comes into the Masters Bedroom with 160 items (the game's own check; nothing before it changes) the ending begins at once. A montage of eight rooms of the mansion, each the game itself running on a new game of its own with its guardians, conveyors and ropes moving as they do and Willy steered by the joystick alone along a way found by playing (re/ending/route.ts), running about the room and taking its items without a life lost; credits over it in the way of Super Mario World's (the game and its makers, true facts about it, this port: a line at a time in the rows between the room's name and the panel, in the title's yellow, where the time and rooms lines are put away; sources in `re/credits.md`); the mansion's guardians walk across the rooms Willy runs through (places they are never in), their own pictures and frames at their own size, each named for a moment in the same rows as it passes the middle (plain names), so every one has been seen by the end; THE END with the items taken, the rooms seen and the game's clock; and back to the title (about a minute in all). The game's own title tune plays throughout; any key or tap skips it after two seconds. Winning is remembered (apart from the saves, so a new game never loses it): from then on W on the pause screen shows it again, and `?ending` on the page shows it at once. A save made in the bedroom does not begin it again. Quality of life for a modern playthrough: a finish worth watching. | ENHANCED |

## Not changed

Everything else is the original: its 136 rooms, items, guardians, the clock and the music. Not built: the original's
practice mode (P on the title, then a room's name to start in) types on the KERNAL's screen editor, which isn't modelled;
P is the pause key instead.

## Proposed

- **Reachability sweep** (not a change): Go-Explore per room from each room's entry, to prove every item cell can be touched
  and find which of the 178 need the Secret Passage trick.
