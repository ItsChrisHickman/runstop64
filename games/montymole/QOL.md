# Wanted: Monty Mole: changes from the original

This remake plays the 1984 ZX Spectrum game (Gremlin Graphics' first, Peter Harrap's) as it was, rebuilt natively from the original's own
data and rules and checked against the original running on a headless Spectrum, pass by pass (`games/montymole/re/README.md`). The game
keeps the Spectrum's own colours in both modes. On top of that sit a few modern quality-of-life additions, listed here. Anything that changes
how the game *plays* or looks (or helps) is in ENHANCED, one switch (ENHANCED by default; ORIGINAL is the game exactly as it was); the rest is in both.

| Feature | The original | In this port | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Title** | The loading picture and a slow Colonel Bogey on the beeper; any key goes on to the options. | The same picture and tune (the tune loops while the title is up). Space (or Z, X, a gamepad's A, a tap) goes on; ENTER CONTINUE (and SPACE NEW GAME) is in the empty row above P.M.HARRAP, in bright white; with no save Enter or a tap also goes on. | Always |
| **Options screen** | `1 KEYBOARD`, `2 KEMPSTON`, `3 SINCLAIR`, `0 PLAY GAME`, in the game's lettering, to pick the controls. | The same screen and lettering with our keys: MOVE: ARROWS OR WASD, JUMP: SPACE Z OR X, GAMEPAD AND TOUCH TOO, P PAUSES AND M MUTES, PRESS JUMP TO PLAY, and the high score. There is nothing to pick: every device works at once. | Always |
| **Controls** | Q up, A down, O left, P right, any key of the bottom right group jumps; or a Kempston or a Sinclair joystick, chosen on the menu. | Arrows or WASD (up and down climb the ropes), Space, Z or X to jump, gamepads. The keys are fixed. | Always |
| **High score** | None (the score is the coal; there is no table). | Kept in your browser, shown on the options screen. | Always |
| **Pause screen** (P, Esc, pad Start, the touch PAUSE button) | None. | The runstop64 pause screen, in the Spectrum's colours and a Spectrum-like font: a map of the 21 rooms, how you're doing (lives, rooms come to, coal taken, score), the options. | Always |
| **Map** | None. | The 21 rooms laid out as the world is (the shaft down the left, the tunnel along the bottom, the caverns, the castle), each a small picture of itself, dark until you have come to it; where you are flashes; a room whose coal is all taken has a green frame; under the grid the picked room and how many of its pieces of coal are taken. | Always |
| **Travel** | None. | From the map, go to any room you have come to: arrows pick, Enter (or jump) goes; on a touch screen tap a room, tap it again to go. You arrive at the place you first came into it. | Always |
| **Back to room start** | None. | B on the pause screen: back to how things were when Monty came into this room (lives, coal and score as then). | Always |
| **Back to the title** | None (only by losing). | T on the pause screen (or a tap on it): the title, with the save kept; ENTER CONTINUE goes back to it, and only a new game started there begins a new one (keeping the save as the previous one). A new game over a save asks first: NEW GAME? | Always |
| **Continue** | Lose the last life: GAME OVER, a key, the options, a new game from nothing (the coal comes back with the opening scene). | GAME OVER plays as it did (the verse typed and scrolled, 510 frames or a key); then the title asks "CONTINUE?" straight away (Enter yes, Esc no, or a tap): Monty is back at the start of the room he was in, with three lives and the coal and score he had. | Always |
| **After the ending** | The verse, a key, the options, a new game from nothing. | The ending's verse plays as it did; then the title, and the save is kept. | Always |
| **Autosave** | None. | Progress is saved in your browser each time Monty enters a room (after a death too: the lives lost stay lost). Launched with a save, the game opens on its title with ENTER CONTINUE, and Enter goes back into play where it was. Starting a new game keeps the save from before it as the previous one: the saved games (F1 on the directory, or the gear there) bring it back. | Always |
| **Dying in the opening scene** | The opening scene (the house, the pit head, the acorn, the water) has an interrupt routine of its own, and it lacks the death animation that ends the death tune early: a death there (the water, a fall off the ground's ends, touching the acorn) leaves the screen frozen for the whole 24-note death tune, about 17 seconds, and then starts the scene again. | A death in the opening scene plays the same rising picture as everywhere (a few seconds) and starts the scene again, with the life gone as anywhere. A bug of the original's, mended in both modes. | Always |
| **Touch controls** (phones, tablets) | None. | 4-way stick and JUMP; PAUSE opens the map (tap a room to pick it, tap it again to travel; tap an option to change it); RETURN key, HOME. Landscape with the controls beside the picture (even with rotation locked), or upright. See docs/mobile.md. | On a touch screen |
| **A moment's grace** | None. | For 3 seconds (150 frames) after the game puts you back into play (continuing a save, back to room start, arriving from the map), a death does not cost a life: Monty is put back where he came into the room, so a resume never starts in the middle of a mishap. No sign on screen. Both modes. | Always |
| **ENHANCED: unlimited lives and continues** | Three lives; the last one lost is GAME OVER. | A death takes no life: the lives stay as they are, so the game never ends and never asks CONTINUE?. He is still put back as the game puts him, and nothing makes him invincible. For a modern playthrough of a classic; the same in every game. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: no deaths from falls** | Landing after a fall of four character rows (32 pixels) or more kills Monty; a drop is something to measure. | A long drop is a way down, not a death: he lands as after a short one. Everything else that kills (the moving things, the crushers, the water and the other deadly colours, the fall out of the bottom of the opening scene's world) still does. For a modern playthrough: ledges can be dropped from without counting rows. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: no colour clash** | A sprite takes the colour of the 8 x 8 cells it is over: Monty and the others change colour as they cross the screen. | The scenery is drawn without the sprites and each sprite whole over it, in one colour: the ink of the cell under its middle for Monty, the colour byte the game gives each thing for the others. Both modes use the Spectrum's own colours. | ENHANCED |
| **ENHANCED: smooth motion** | One logic step in two frames (25 a second) in the rooms, one in a frame in the opening scene, on a 50 Hz television. | The logic is the original's; the sprites glide evenly across each step and sideways between frames on 60/120/144 Hz screens. Up and down they are drawn where the game puts them, so a figure stands on its floor. V switches to whole-pixel steps. | ENHANCED |

## Not changed

Everything else is the original: the 21 rooms and their coal, objects and gates (16, 32, 48 and 64 pieces), the opening scene, Monty's walk, jump,
fall and ropes, every thing that moves, the crushers, the retractable floors, the conveyors, three lives, the score (the coal's worth, as the
programmer's source leaves it), the graphics, the death, GAME OVER and the ending's verse. The sound is the original's too, from the beeper's own
timing: the title and death tunes, the footsteps, the thud and the pick-ups; M mutes.
