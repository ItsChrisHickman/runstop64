# The next Monty game (notes, not started)

Chris, 2026-11-27, while Do Svidaniya Monty was being finished. Not started: Do Svidaniya ships first.

## Premise (agreed in outline)

The whole game takes place on **Montos**, Monty's Greek island (where Monty on the Run ended), not another world tour.

- **The deal (Chris):** Monty is pardoned for the bucket of coal, but the pardon is a bargain: in exchange he signs over the
  mining rights to Montos to the same coal company from Wanted: Monty Mole. The irony: the stolen bucket of coal ends with the
  coal company digging up his own island.
- **Something bad has happened in the mine (Chris).** Open: what. Candidates (proposed 2026-11-27):
  - The miners dug too deep and went silent; the mine is still running by itself (lifts, coal trucks on rails, Mole
    Crushers working with no one at the controls), and the company says it is Monty's island, so Monty's problem: the pardon
    holds only if he sorts it out.
  - Deep down they broke into the ancient Labyrinth under the island and let its residents out: the Minotaur, harpies, a
    cyclops, Medusa's stare (turned to stone for a few seconds), Zeus's thunderbolts on a cycle. Fresh enemies and
    Mediterranean colours, nothing reused from Moscow-to-Rio.
  - The digging woke the island's volcano: the bottom of the map, the last part.

## Shape

One deep connected place, as Monty on the Run's, cut away like an ant farm: the surface (village, docks, the company's
winding towers on his beach), the working mine, the Labyrinth, the volcano at the bottom. It goes down, not across: a game
for a mole. Ending idea: the mine sealed, the island his again, and this time he chooses to stay.

Rejected for now: a resort Montos (mostly a surface game), Monty's family moving in (no enemy), a USA/Mexico/Canada tour, space,
time travel (Chris: "contrived").

Start from `.claude/skills/new-monty-game/SKILL.md` and `tools/new-game.mjs` when it begins.
