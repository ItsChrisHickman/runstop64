# Do Svidaniya Monty: the plan

A new Monty game, made in 2026 on Monty on the Run's engine, rules and parts. Chris asked for it on 2026-11-20; this page is
the agreed shape. It is built a region at a time, and Chris plays each one before the next is started.

## The story

Intermole finds Monty's Greek island (where Auf Wiedersehen Monty left him) and Europe is no longer safe. Intermole only
works in Europe, so he runs east and out of its reach, the long way round, with no passport: through Moscow, across Siberia
on the Trans-Siberian railway, through China, by junk to Japan, by cargo ship to Hawaii, stowed in a cargo plane's hold
over the Pacific, by parachute down into the Andes at Machu Picchu, by riverboat down the Amazon, to Rio de Janeiro. Every
leg is a different way of travelling. The game ends on Rio's beach under Christ the Redeemer, eating an açaí bowl (Brazil's own: the berry is from the Amazon and the bowl a Rio beach food before surfers took it to Hawaii), retired again, out of reach for
good.

**The name** (Chris, 2026-11-20): Do Svidaniya Monty, Russian for goodbye, as Auf Wiedersehen Monty is German for it (that
one was named after the TV series Auf Wiedersehen, Pet). "Rio or Bust" and "From Russia with Mole" were turned down as the
title; Rio or Bust can name a room. No James Bond puns: Monty doesn't do them.

A sequel (later, not now) has the Crown Jewels stolen and scattered, and a pardon offered if Monty brings them back: from
Rio up through Central America and Mexico, the US, New York, the Atlantic, the Tower, and home to his Yorkshire village. The
two games together go once round the world.

## The world

Seven regions of about seven rooms each (the Pacific about fourteen), about fifty-five rooms in all (Monty on the Run has 52). The regions follow each other west
to east on the exit grid, so the pause screen's map reads as the trip round the world.

**Inside a region the way is not a line** (Chris, 2026-11-20: what he liked most in Monty on the Run). Each region is a block
of rooms several high, not a row: start going one way, go down, circle back up; pass through a room and on through others
before doubling back to the ladder that leads out of it; rooms entered from more than one side, a dead end with an item, a
way that only opens once an item is found elsewhere. Only the region's entry and its exit to the next region are fixed
points. The model is Monty on the Run's own route (study its exit grid and how its rooms join), not Auf
Wiedersehen Monty's: Chris finds the first game's flow far more interesting than the sequel's open map and flights.

## Lessons from Chris's play (read before designing any region)

- **A place must feel like the thing, not be rooms labelled as it** (Chris on Siberia's train, 2026-11-21: "it just looks like
  rooms with words that say their carts"). A vehicle is shown as itself: a train is a side view of its carriages on the rails
  (bogies and wheels under each car, the body's window row, the curved roof, the bellows between cars), walked along car by car,
  with the landscape moving past in its windows and under it; boarding it is a moment (the platform, the door, then it moves). The
  same goes for every vehicle and set piece: the junk, the bullet train, the cargo ship, the cargo plane's hold, the riverboat,
  Sugarloaf's cable car. Signs name a place; they never stand in for drawing it. The level is Monty on the Run's: it leaves
  a lot to the imagination, but its boat looks like a boat and its C5 street like a road (Chris): read at a glance, simply drawn.

## The rules (Monty on the Run's)

- **Monty himself is Monty on the Run's:** his walk, his jump arc, ropes and ladders, no steering in the air, coins (50) and
  items (200), the deaths, lives.
- **Kept from Monty on the Run** (Chris, 2026-11-20): the Mole Crushers and their quirks, lifts, water and its fish, clouds,
  and a flying section like the jetpack's (the most fun part).
- **Left out:** the timed warps (wait for the moment you may walk through, or be sent back: frustrating), and a vehicle
  section like the C5 street.
- **New things that feel familiar but fresh:** each region adds one or two mechanisms of its own, made the way Monty on
  the Run's are (a timing to learn, seen at once, fair): Moscow's escalators, icicles that fall when he passes under them;
  the Trans-Siberian's carriage roofs and the tunnels that sweep them; firecrackers and the long New Year dragon in China;
  the bullet train sweeping a platform in Japan; swinging cargo on the ship's cranes; rising lava on the volcano; a flight
  the parachute drop from the plane into the Andes as the jetpack section: Auf Wiedersehen Monty's flight
  idea redeemed (Chris: its minigame was terrible), flying room by room as the jetpack does, down through the mountains past
  condors and updrafts to a landing at Machu Picchu. Each is proposed to Chris with its region before it is built.
- **A hat or costume in each country** (Chris, 2026-11-20): found in the region, it is that region's key item and Monty
  wears it from then on (his own frames with it drawn on, until the next region's): the ushanka in Moscow, a rice hat in
  China, a lei in Hawaii, and so on. It lets him blend in: the region's guard or gate lets him by once he wears it.
- **A luau in Hawaii** (Chris: "for sure"): Hawaii's high point is a luau room (the fire dancers' torches as its timing, the
  roast pig, the lei), and it comes back in the reward show.
- **Each region's food in place of the cupcakes** (Chris, 2026-11-20): the bonus food (200 points, as the cake) is local:
  pirozhki in Moscow, dumplings in China, sushi in Japan, Spam musubi in Hawaii (a generic tin, no logo), empanadas in the
  Andes, churrasco skewers in Rio (Chris: Brazil means the Brazilian steakhouse; a food must be recognisable). Rio may get a churrascaria room: gaucho waiters (Chris thinks of gauchos; they're from Brazil's far south, the churrasco's home) carrying long skewers between the tables, their swinging skewers the timing. Coconut shrimp hop off the luau's platters as creatures, and a
  shrimp shack stands on Waikiki with its sign.
- **C64 references** (Chris, 2026-11-20), small homages with nothing copied, only to games Chris played and loves:
  WILLOW PATTERN (China, the room's exact name), THE LAST MOLE (Japan), Jeff Minter's llamas winking at Machu Picchu. Turned
  down: Tetris, Bruce Lee, Pitfall, Impossible Mission, Boulder Dash, California Games (games he doesn't know, or stretches),
  anything Pendragon.
- **Items open the way.** As in Monty on the Run, an item taken in one room changes another (a wall gone, a creature seen
  off): two or three in each region, some needed in the next region. No freedom kit to pick (the remake already dropped it).
  An item's use has hint text in ENHANCED, as in Auf Wiedersehen Monty.
- **ORIGINAL and ENHANCED** as in every game: ORIGINAL is the game as made (lives, game over); ENHANCED adds what Monty on the
  Run's ENHANCED has (unlimited lives and continues, no death loops, the hints, the reward show). Map, travel, autosave and
  back to room start are always on.
- **The look:** Monty on the Run's tiles, sprites and decorations where they fit, and new ones in its style (its tile size,
  colours and sprite shapes) for each region's places and creatures. Place names on signs like its MPL ST. sign.
- **The music:** Rob Hubbard's Monty on the Run tune and sound effects, played by his own driver. Each region gets its own
  version of the tune (Chris, 2026-11-20), made by changing the driver's data: the melody bent to the region's scale (minor
  for Russia, the pentatonic for China and Japan), instruments that suggest the place (a plucked balalaika, bells, a sliding
  ukulele), and maybe a short phrase of a public-domain folk tune as a break (Kalinka, Sakura). Moscow's version
  comes with Moscow's rooms; if it sounds like a gimmick, every region keeps the original. The tempo stays Hubbard's (Chris).
- **The title:** Monty on the Run's title screen, with MONTY MOLE: WORLD TOUR and the story on its scroller. Credits: a new
  game by Christopher Hickman with Claude, after Peter Harrap's Monty on the Run (Gremlin Graphics, 1985).

## How it's built

- **One engine.** Monty on the Run's engine (`games/montyotr/src`) takes its game data as a value instead of importing the
  original's, so Do Svidaniya Monty (`games/montyworld`) is a second data set on the same code, not a copy. Monty on the Run plays
  exactly as before: its tests, the frame-for-frame title check included, all pass unchanged.
- **Rooms in the original's own terms,** as runstop64's rooms already are (`games/montyotr/src/data/runstop64-rooms.ts`:
  playfield maps, tiles, coins, decorations, crushers, teleporters, enemies).
- **Proved by the bot:** every room crossed, every coin and item reachable, the game finishable, in both modes.
- In the directory (listed once Chris played Moscow), at `#montyworld`.

## Steps

1. The engine takes its data as a value (Monty on the Run unchanged), and Do Svidaniya Monty's skeleton loads. Done: `MontyData`
   (`games/montyotr/src/montydata.ts`), Monty on the Run's value `motr.ts`, Do Svidaniya Monty's `games/montyworld/src/data.ts` (50 by 3
   exit grid, three placeholder rooms), the session `runMonty` (`session.ts`) and `test/walk.ts`. The pause map draws the wide grid
   in cells of 3 pixels for now: its design for a strip of fifty rooms (scrolling, or regions) is part of step 2.
2. Moscow: its nine rooms (`docs/dosvidaniya-moscow.md`, approved 2026-11-20), art, creatures and item puzzles, playable from the title to the
   Trans-Siberian's carriage roof. Built: the nine rooms (`games/montyworld/src/moscow.ts`), eleven creatures (none in more than two rooms),
   escalators and icicles as engine features (data: `escalators`, `icicles`), the metro token and the ushanka (a costume: Monty's
   pictures with the hat on, kept in the save), pirozhki for cakes, ENHANCED item hints, its own save codec, a pause map that shows the
   region he is in; the bot (`games/montyworld/test/moscow.ts`) plays it from the start to the roof's end in ORIGINAL and ENHANCED.
   The way east off the carriage roof is shut with planks and a sign (SIBERIA COMING SOON) until Siberia is built. Done 2026-11-20; as built: `docs/dosvidaniya-moscow.md`.
   **Chris plays it.** If it doesn't feel like a Monty game, we stop here.
3. Siberia: nine rooms built (2026-11-21; `docs/dosvidaniya-siberia.md`): the train's roofs and cars with tunnel mouths, the pine forest and the yeti's hollow, the mine with its cart, lamp and dark shaft, Baikal's cracking ice, the border village; its tune, pelmeni, hints, bot test and mechanism tests. **Chris plays it** before China.

4. The ending on Rio's beach, the reward show, the credits.
